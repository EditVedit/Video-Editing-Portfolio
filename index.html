About

Hey, I’m Vishal — Video Editor & Storyteller.

I believe a great video doesn’t just grab attention. It makes people feel something, remember something, and sometimes, even take action.

My journey into video editing wasn’t exactly traditional. I didn’t have a degree or a fancy qualification to back me up. Just curiosity, creativity, and a genuine love for storytelling.

That passion helped me earn my place at DEV IT, where I eventually grew into a Senior Video Editor.

Today, I help brands and businesses turn their ideas into stories that connect with people. Because to me, editing isn’t just about making videos look good. It’s about understanding what a business wants to communicate and making sure its audience actually cares.
